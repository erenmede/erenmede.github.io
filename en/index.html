Why me

A worksheet only works when the student can actually get to it. I have spent twenty years as an English teacher and vice principal in a Turkish state school, on the receiving end of Ministry of National Education materials, so I know how a document behaves during a lesson. I run my accessibility work the same way: the PAC report should come back clean, and the student should still be able to do the activity.

I know educational content from the inside

Worksheets, test questions, diagrams and charts. Because I know what they are used for in class, I write alt text and set reading order around what the student has to do.

I work in two languages

I prepare Turkish documents so that they are read as Turkish (document language, title, metadata). I can deliver my reports in Turkish or in English.

I measure the user, not just the report

Tools like PAC check structure; they cannot tell whether alt text is useful. In every project I list separately what a tool verified and what is my own judgement.

Why now

Presidential Circular 2025/10, published in the Official Gazette on 21 June 2025, asks a wide range of organisations to bring their websites and mobile apps in line with WCAG 2.2 within one year. These include public institutions, universities, municipalities, banks and private schools licensed by the Ministry of National Education. Worksheets, announcements and forms published as PDFs on those sites are web content covered by WCAG. In the EU, the European Accessibility Act has applied since 28 June 2025.

What I do

PDF/UA remediation

Tag tree, reading order, heading hierarchy, table headers, alt text, document language and title. The target standard is PDF/UA-1 (ISO 14289-1).

PAC audit and report

A PAC 2024 report before and after the work. I separate errors from warnings, and write down what each warning means and why I left it.

Materials with activities

For activities such as placing terms on a diagram or matching, I propose an equivalent that lets a student using a screen reader show the same skill.
